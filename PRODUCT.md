# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Learners practice structured case solving and develop evidence of their problem-solving ability. Employers and colleges are intended audiences for credible assessment and curriculum use.

## Product Purpose

Cracked? is a case-solving platform where people practice on cases and questions, submit structured answers, compare approaches with other learners, and build a record of their work.

## Positioning

The long-term goal is to make demonstrated problem-solving ability measurable and trusted enough to support hiring and education decisions. Case submissions are structurally validated and solutions can be compared after submitting; broader institutional trust is an ambition, not an established outcome.

## Operating Context

Learners browse cases, questions, and open practice activities; submit answers; compare other case solutions after submitting; and view profiles and platform scores. Companies can post cases through a separate product flow.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The web app includes case, question, and open-arena practice; structured answer submissions; solution comparison; platform scoring, leaderboards, profiles, certificates, and company case posting. Case solutions are available to their author and case proposer, and are anonymized in the comparison view. Signed-in contributors can submit structured case proposals for moderator review. Approved proposals are published to the case library; private reviewer guidance is not published. Moderators are configured with the server-side `CASE_MODERATOR_EMAILS` allowlist. Authentication and protected routes are part of the product.

AI-assisted answer verification, adaptive follow-up questioning, college curriculum adoption, creator course sales, and additional industry verticals are future possibilities, not current capabilities. Do not imply that employers or colleges already rely on Cracked? scores.

## Brand Commitments

The product is named Cracked? The user wants it to feel minimalist, professional, readable, and credible, with refined boxes and buttons. The primary audience for the current landing-page pass should be presented inclusively across learners, employers, and colleges.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains working product routes and workflows. No verified employer adoption, college partnerships, testimonials, or independently validated score benchmarks have been provided. Do not fabricate social proof or claim external validation.

## Product Principles

- Make problem-solving practice structured and useful.
- Treat scores as evidence that must earn trust; do not overstate their current validation.
- Let learners form their own solution before revealing other approaches.
- Publish community case proposals only after moderator review; keep confidential interview and proprietary source material out of submissions.
- Present future AI, institutional, creator, and industry-expansion ideas as vision until implemented.
